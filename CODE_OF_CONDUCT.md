# Code Of Conduct

Hello Solar Planner is built for communities and by a broad mix of practitioners. Project spaces must be welcoming, practical, and safe for participation.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, location, experience, or role.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical and safety concerns.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Credit contributors and community knowledge appropriately.
- Protect private, operational, and site-specific information.
- Make space for contributors who are new to software, solar planning, or open-source work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately unsafe technical advice or removal of required safety warnings.
- Repeated disruption of issues, reviews, or community discussion.

## Reporting And Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through a confidential GitLab issue or the established Project Hello World contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or take other proportionate action when this code is violated.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, respond consistently, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
