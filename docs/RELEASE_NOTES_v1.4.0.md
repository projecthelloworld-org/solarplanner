# Hello Solar Planner v1.4.0

Released 29 September 2026. The hosted variant is tagged `v1.4.0-phw`.

## Equipment choices and capacity fit

- Choose from more sourced panel, LiFePO4 battery and MPPT controller sizes, including smaller equipment for light loads.
- Select a panel reference alongside battery and controller references, with capacity and unit price shown in each choice.
- Generated plans compare complete equipment combinations. Among compatible plans within 10% of the cheapest equipment cost, selection favors the smallest combined proportional excess in panel capacity, battery energy and controller output. Existing safety margins remain included.
- See required capacity, installed capacity and excess percentages in the dashboard, PDF and CSV.
- Distinguish named product prices, representative size estimates, user quotations and unverified allowances. Representative prices do not verify electrical specifications.
- Unverified equipment checks start collapsed; failed checks and overall status remain visible.

## Existing projects

Saved quotations and catalogue identities are preserved. No saved-project migration is needed. Calculate regenerates both equipment options while retaining explicit quotations, so recommendations and totals may change with the expanded catalogue and selection method. Review retained quotations when equipment changes.

Prices are indicative African market planning estimates. A ±10% variation around the listed estimates may be used for initial budgeting; this is a planning assumption, not a measured market range or a verified comparison with global averages. Actual costs may fall outside this range. Confirm current local quotations before procurement.

The optional budgeting variation does not automatically change totals. It is separate from both the equipment selection window and the contingency allowance.

## Validation

Automated coverage includes capacity fit, sizing boundaries, voltage and topology constraints, integrated charging, quotations, persistence and report consistency. Desktop and mobile selector workflows and representative PDF/CSV exports were checked during implementation.
